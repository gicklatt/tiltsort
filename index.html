A few little details.

Where can I play?

Tilt Sort is available now for iPhone and iPad on the App Store, and for Android on Google Play. Download it free using the links above.

How do the controls work?

Swipe in one of four directions. Every box slides together until something stops it. Plan your moves to deliver each target's colors in order.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The puzzles work offline, with no account required and no in-app purchases.

Are there ads?

Version 1.0 has no ads. The planned 2.0 update introduces optional rewarded ads for hints and anchors for eligible players aged 13+. Watching an ad requires internet; regular gameplay works offline. Check the store page for the version available to you.
